# Vikas Rajput

Personal website focused on **Cloud | Data & AI | Engineering Leadership**, published at <https://vikasrajput.github.io>.

## SEO and content

Shared titles, canonical URLs, social previews and JSON-LD are generated by `jekyll-seo-tag` through `_includes/meta.html`. Site identity, author details and the default preview image are configured in `_config.yml`.

Give each page and post a descriptive `title` and a unique `description` in its YAML front matter. The homepage uses the site title and positioning tagline. Keep existing permalinks when changing article titles so published links continue to work.

Layouts supply the page's H1. Start article sections at H2 and subsections at H3. Posts show their publication date; add `last_modified_at` with the actual revision date when an article is substantively updated.

GitHub Pages generates the sitemap and robots.txt through `jekyll-sitemap`. The 404 page is excluded from the sitemap and marked `noindex`.

After publishing, submit <https://vikasrajput.github.io/sitemap.xml> in Google Search Console and inspect the homepage and key article URLs. Search Console verification and analytics account configuration are managed separately; no verification token or replacement analytics ID is included here.
